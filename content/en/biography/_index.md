        # Media Biography

        ## Short Official Biography

        **Alexey Kuzmenkov is a physician-scientist, Doctor of Medical Sciences, Professor, MBA, and science entrepreneur specializing in clinical pharmacology, antimicrobial therapy, microbiology, antimicrobial resistance, antimicrobial susceptibility testing, and medical technology.**

        - Co-founder and Chief Science and Innovation Officer of ABioGram
        - Founder of the open AMRhub science and education ecosystem
        - Deputy Director for Strategic Development at the Institute of Antimicrobial Chemotherapy and Professor of Microbiology at Smolensk State Medical University

        He is the author and co-author of more than 100 scientific publications, textbooks, and practical guidelines, as well as 17 patents and other intellectual property assets.
